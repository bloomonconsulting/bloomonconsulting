Nice to meet you

I’m Malak, an operations consultant for small businesses. My work starts with learning what you do and how your current systems work, including how long each task takes.

From there, I build more efficient ways of doing the work. I want to understand your business well enough that what I build makes life easier for you and your staff, instead of becoming one more platform to learn.

A lot of what I build is custom, so it’s tailored to what you need and can be automated. I keep your monthly invoices in mind too, and I use what you already pay for whenever it does the job.

I come from people work. I didn’t do it for the money. I did it for the people, and that’s still how I approach everything: people first. I’m thoughtful about your needs and flexible in how we work together, because trust comes from knowing I’m invested in the success of you and your business.

I especially love working with owners who are still doing things by hand. I set up the system and walk you through the easiest way to use it, step by step. Along the way, I point out features that already come with what you’re paying for.

My services are for businesses that want to grow without a huge budget or a big staff, including owners who run everything themselves. I’ve spent years building operations and systems for small teams and mission-driven organizations.

Once your systems are running, you can stay connected with me so I can keep them updated and fix anything that needs it. I’m based in the Dallas-Fort Worth area and work with small businesses remotely across the US.
