Save the email you type every week in Gmail

Turn on templates and reuse any email in two clicks.

← All tips

If you send the same welcome email, follow-up or invoice reminder over and over, Gmail can save it as a template. Templates stay off until you switch them on.

Steps

  1. In Gmail on a computer, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. Open the Advanced tab. Next to Templates, click Enable, then Save Changes.
  3. Click Compose and write the email you want to reuse.
  4. Click the three dots at the bottom of the draft, then Templates, Save draft as template, Save as new template.
  5. Give it a name you'll recognize and click Save.

Good to know

To use it, start a new email, click the three dots, then Templates, and pick yours. Add the person's name and any details, then send.

To update a template, write the new version, then choose Save draft as template and pick the template's name to overwrite it.
