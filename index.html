I work with owners who are still doing things by hand and want step-by-step help with new tools. Here are some signs it’s time.

Signs it’s time

  • Admin takes up more of your week than the work you started your business to do.
  • Onboarding a new client looks different every time.
  • Invoices and follow-ups only happen when you remember them.
  • Your tools don’t share information with each other.
  • Important details live in your head instead of somewhere your team can find them.
