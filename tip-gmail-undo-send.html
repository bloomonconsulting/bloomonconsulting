How to unsend an email in Gmail

Give yourself 30 seconds to take back an email.

← All tips

Gmail holds each email for a few seconds before it goes out, so you can click Undo if you catch a mistake. The default is 5 seconds, and you can change it to 30.

Steps

  1. Open Gmail on a computer and click the gear icon in the top right.
  2. Click See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, find Undo Send.
  4. Set the Send cancellation period to 30 seconds.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Good to know

Now an Undo button shows after you hit Send. Click it and your email opens back up as a draft, so you can fix it or delete it.

The setting follows your account, so it works in the Gmail phone app too.
