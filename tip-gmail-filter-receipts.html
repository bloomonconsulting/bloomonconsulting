Sort your receipts automatically in Gmail

Label receipts as they arrive, ready for tax time.

← All tips

A Gmail filter can label receipts as they arrive, so they're all in one place at tax time.

Steps

  1. In the Gmail search bar, click the search options icon on the right.
  2. In Has the words, type receipt, then click Create filter.
  3. Check Apply the label, choose New label, and call it Receipts.
  4. Check Also apply filter to matching conversations to sort the ones already in your inbox.
  5. Click Create filter.

Good to know

You'll find them anytime under Receipts in the left menu.

To catch more, edit the filter and search for receipt OR invoice. You can make the same kind of filter for any vendor by putting their email address in the From field.
