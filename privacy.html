Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, how it is used, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers bloomonconsulting.com, run by Malak Baker as Bloom On Consulting, based in Texas. If you have a question about anything here, email malak@bloomonconsulting.com.

Information you share with me

Booking a consultation. The booking button opens Google Calendar appointment scheduling. The name, email address and any notes you enter there go to my Google account so I can confirm and prepare for our call. Google handles that page under its own privacy policy.

Emailing me. When you email me, I keep the message and your contact details so I can reply and follow up on your request.

Free resources. When you sign up for a free resource, I collect your name and email address. They are stored in a Google Sheet in my business Google account so I can send you the resource. I will only send you other emails if you agree to them when you sign up, and every email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Information collected automatically

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and how they find the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect details such as pages viewed, time on the site, device and browser type, and approximate location based on your IP address. I do not use this information to identify you, and advertising features are turned off.

You can turn analytics off for this site with the cookie notice at the bottom of the page. If you already made a choice, you can change it here:

You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or block cookies in your browser settings.

How I use your information

I use it to respond to you, schedule and prepare for consultations, deliver work and resources you ask for, and improve this website. I do not sell or rent your information, and I do not share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

I rely on a few trusted services to run my business, and they process information on my behalf: Google (Calendar, Gmail, Sheets and Analytics), IONOS for business email, and GitHub Pages, which hosts this site and may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. I may also share information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep contact and booking details for as long as we are in touch or working together, plus any time needed for tax or legal records. Free resource signups stay on my list until you unsubscribe or ask me to remove you. Google Analytics deletes its data automatically after the retention period set in my account.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information I have about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email malak@bloomonconsulting.com with your request and I will respond within 30 days.

Security

This site uses HTTPS, and I limit access to your information to the accounts I use to run Bloom On. Please keep sensitive details such as passwords or payment information out of email and booking notes.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I will change the date at the top of this page.
