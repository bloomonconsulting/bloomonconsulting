Send client intake answers to one spreadsheet

Every new client's answers land as a new row.

← All tips

A Google Form can be your intake form, and every answer shows up as a new row in Google Sheets.

Steps

  1. Go to forms.google.com and start a blank form.
  2. Add the questions you ask every new client, like their name, email, phone number and what they need help with.
  3. Open the Responses tab and click Link to Sheets.
  4. Choose Create a new spreadsheet, then Create.
  5. Click Publish, then copy the responder link.

Good to know

Share the link with new clients or add it to your website. New answers show up in the spreadsheet on their own.

Send yourself a test response first so you can see how it looks.
