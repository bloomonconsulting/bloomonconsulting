Share one booking link from Google Calendar

Let people pick a time from one link.

← All tips

Google Calendar has a booking page you can share as one link. People pick an open time, and it lands on your calendar. A free Google account includes one booking page, and paid Google Workspace plans add more.

Steps

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Click Create, then Appointment schedule.
  3. Give it a title. This is what people see on your booking page.
  4. Set the meeting length and your available hours, then click Next.
  5. Check your booking page details and click Save.

Good to know

To share it, click the schedule on your calendar and copy the booking page link. Paste it into your emails or your email signature.

You can only create a booking page from a computer, but you can share the link from anywhere.
