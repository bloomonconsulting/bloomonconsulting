Type @@ and your email address appears

Turn a short code into anything you type often.

← All tips

Your phone can turn a short code into anything you type often, like your email address or your booking link.

Steps

  1. On iPhone, open Settings, then General, then Keyboard.
  2. Tap Text Replacement, then the plus sign.
  3. Put your email address in Phrase and @@ in Shortcut.
  4. Tap Save.

Good to know

On Android with Gboard, open Gboard settings, then Dictionary, Personal dictionary, and your language. Tap the plus sign and add your email with a shortcut like @@. Menus can vary by phone.

Now type @@ in any app and your phone offers your full email address.
