Turn messy notes into a follow-up email

Get a clean follow-up email from your notes.

← All tips

After a call, paste your notes into an AI chat tool like ChatGPT or Claude and ask for the follow-up email.

Steps

  1. Open your AI chat tool and paste your notes.
  2. Paste this prompt under them: “Turn these notes into a short, friendly follow-up email to my client. List the next steps and who is doing each one. Keep it under 150 words.”
  3. Read what it writes and change anything that doesn’t sound like you.
  4. Copy it into your email and send.

Good to know

Leave private details like account numbers out of what you paste.

Save the prompt in your notes app so it's ready after every call.
